# Mean Girls (2004)
## About the movie 
Mean girls is a comedy movie about high school life, friendship, popularity,and peer pressure. It follows Cady Heron as she enters a new school and became friends with a popular called the Plastics.
## Movie Details 
- **Title:** Mean Girls
- **Release Year:** 2004
- **Genre:** Comedy, Teen
- **Director:** Mark Waters
## Main Characters
1. Cady Heron
2. Regina George
3. Gretchen Wieners
4. Karen Smith
5. Janis Ian
6. Damian Leigh
## Why I Like This Movie
